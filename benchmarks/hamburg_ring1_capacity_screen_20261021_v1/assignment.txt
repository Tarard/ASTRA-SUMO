The collisions are a separate, mechanical problem.

Every lane-intent waypoint ends 0.01 m before the stop line.
All 50 collisions occur on the junction path immediately downstream of such a waypoint: 46 at LSA104 (139083723#0 lane 2, right turn) and 4 at LSA119 (K6 left).
At 104 the colliding vehicles are consecutive vehicles of the same route (e.g. p208_v16 → v17).
SUMO’s stopped counter going negative points the same way: SUMO handles waypoints badly where they meet a signal stop line.
Next assignment: one corrective iteration, two declared changes

The two changes act on symptoms that can be told apart (collision locations versus insertion per entry), so one run tests both.

For every signalled link on each candidate route, add the constraint: flow per 15-min bin ≤ 1,800 veh/h × lanes × (green / cycle) × 0.8. Take the green share from the installed programs, for official and placeholder signals alike. Declare the 0.8 factor; it allows for start-up losses and closely spaced uncoordinated signals.
